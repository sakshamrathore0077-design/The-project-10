Security

Your security is our priority

We invest heavily in cutting-edge security infrastructure so you can bank with complete peace of mind.

End-to-End Encryption

All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Your financial information is never stored in plain text.

Multi-Factor Authentication

Biometrics, SMS codes, authenticator apps, and hardware keys — choose the method that suits you best.

Real-time Monitoring

AI systems monitor every transaction 24/7, flagging suspicious activity instantly and protecting your account proactively.

Secure Key Infrastructure

Keys are stored in FIPS 140-2 Level 3 compliant Hardware Security Modules, ensuring the highest level of key protection.

Regulatory Compliance

Fully licensed and regulated by financial authorities. SOC 2 Type II certified with regular third-party security audits.
