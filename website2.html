How long does the battery last?

AURA One delivers up to 48 hours of playback with ANC off, and 36 hours with ANC on. A quick 10-minute charge gives you 5 hours of listening.

Is it water resistant?

Yes, AURA One is IP68 rated, meaning it's fully dustproof and can be submerged in water up to 2 meters for 30 minutes.

What devices are compatible?

AURA One works with any Bluetooth 5.3+ device. For spatial audio features, iOS 17+ or Android 14+ is required.

What's the return policy?

We offer a 30-day no-questions-asked return policy. Free return shipping included in all orders.

How do I pair my AURA One?

Open the charging case near your phone, and the AURA app will guide you through pairing. Bluetooth pairing is also available through your device settings.

How do I reset my device?

Hold both earbuds for 15 seconds until the LED flashes amber. Then place them back in the case for 10 seconds.

What warranty do you offer?

All AURA products come with a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Extended warranty available for purchase.

How do I update firmware?

Open the AURA app, go to Settings > Firmware Update. Make sure your earbuds are charged above 50% and connected via Bluetooth.
