About This Property

Nestled in the exclusive Bel Air neighborhood, Villa Aurora is a masterpiece of contemporary architecture blended with timeless Mediterranean elegance. This extraordinary estate spans 12,500 square feet of meticulously crafted living space, featuring soaring ceilings, walls of glass that dissolve indoor-outdoor boundaries, and finishes of the finest caliber.

The grand foyer opens to reveal a sweeping double staircase, while the chef's kitchen features Italian marble countertops and Gaggenau appliances. The primary suite is a private retreat with a spa-like bathroom, dual walk-in closets, and a terrace overlooking the infinity pool and canyon views.

About Aurum

Established 1989

A Legacy of
Exceptional Service

For over three decades, Aurum Estates has been the definitive name in luxury real estate. Founded on the principle that extraordinary properties deserve extraordinary representation, we have built a reputation for discretion, expertise, and results that speak for themselves.

From the sunlit villas of the Côte d'Azur to the soaring penthouses of Manhattan, our global network of specialists connects discerning clients with properties that transcend the ordinary. Every transaction is handled with the care and confidentiality that our clientele demands.

We don't just sell homes—we curate legacies. Our approach combines deep market intelligence with an unwavering commitment to personal service, ensuring that each client's journey is as exceptional as the property they acquire.
